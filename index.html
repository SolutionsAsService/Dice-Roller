    Formula

    Formula syntax
    • 2d6+3 – two d6 plus 3
    • d20, d% – one die (d% = d100)
    • 4d6kh3 / 4d6dl1 – keep highest 3
    • 2d20kl1 – keep lowest 1
    • d8+3d6-1 – mix groups and modifiers

      Dice bag

      Keep up to 20 of each die, each in its own numbered slot with its own name, look and stats. Dice roll in slot order unless you press Use to send particular dice first; roll more than you own and grey house dice fill in. ✎ renames and restyles a die, ★ marks your lucky die, and the flame burns a traitor.

            Burn this die?

            It goes to the Hall of Shame with its stats, and a brand-new die takes its place. Rolls stay perfectly fair either way – this is purely for catharsis. You can pardon it later.
